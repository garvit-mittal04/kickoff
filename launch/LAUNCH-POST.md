## POST BODY

60 days ago I signed up for the AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge with zero product-building experience.

Today I'm launching my capstone.

It's called Kickoff.

The problem it solves:

Every analyst and ops person I've ever worked with has been handed a vague exec question like "why is retention down?" or "should we launch feature X?" — and spent the next four hours guessing what was really being asked.

The exec wanted a decision. The analyst delivered a dashboard. The scope was never aligned. Everyone was frustrated.

Kickoff fixes that upstream.

You paste the vague exec question. It runs a 5-minute AI-powered interview to probe baseline, segments, definitions, and downstream decisions. Then it produces a structured analysis brief you can send to the exec BEFORE you touch the warehouse.

Sub-questions ranked. Hypotheses stack-ranked. Data sources named. Success criteria stated.

Basically: the scoping document you always wished existed, in 5 minutes, for free.

Try it right now (link in comments):
→ Nonprofit retention drop → /b/retention
→ SaaS Q3 revenue variance → /b/revenue
→ Product launch go/no-go → /b/launch

Or paste your own real exec question — it takes ~5 minutes.

The whole thing runs on Cloudflare Pages + Workers + Workers AI (Llama 3.2 3B) + KV. Total monthly cost: $0.00. No signup, no API key, no PII. The permalink is the product.

10 days ago it was a Figma sketch. Today it's live software solving a real problem for a real audience.

What 60 days of building with Claude taught me:

1. You don't need to be a "developer" to ship real products anymore. You need product taste, a clear user, and the discipline to show up daily.

2. The right tooling collapses the loop. Claude turns "I have an idea" into "there's a working prototype by end of day" in a way that just wasn't true 18 months ago.

3. Building in public is a forcing function. Knowing I had to post something every day meant I couldn't rabbit-hole. Every day had to produce a real deliverable.

4. Free-tier infrastructure is now enterprise-grade. This entire product runs on infrastructure that costs nothing. The cost floor for launching real software has essentially dropped to zero.

Massive thanks to @Anil Bajpai for designing the challenge and pushing this cohort every single day. And to @Anthropic for building the model that made this possible.

If you're an analyst, ops person, PM, or founder who's been on either side of the "vague exec question" problem — try Kickoff and tell me what breaks. I want the feedback.

Day 60 of 60. Capstone shipped. Onwards.
