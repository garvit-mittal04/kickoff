# Direct Outreach Templates — Day 60

Goal: 15 personal DMs on launch day (5 per template). Not blast. Personal.

Every message ends with a specific ask. Vague messages get vague responses.

---

## TEMPLATE A — Close friends / former teammates (5 people)

Send via WhatsApp / iMessage / Signal. Casual tone. No formality.

```
hey — launching the thing today

it's called Kickoff, it turns vague exec questions ("why is retention down?")
into a structured analysis brief in 5 mins. free, no signup.

live here: https://kickoff-5r0.pages.dev
example: https://kickoff-5r0.pages.dev/b/retention

if you have 5 mins today, would love a quick reaction — does the brief format
actually feel useful, or does it feel like AI slop? honest take is more useful
than a like.

and if it's genuinely useful to you, a share would be huge on launch day 🙏
```

**Ask:** honest reaction + a share if genuine.

---

## TEMPLATE B — Warm reconnects (5 people you haven't spoken to in 3-12 months)

Send via LinkedIn DM. Slightly more context up front because they don't have full backstory.

```
Hey [Name] — hope you've been well.

Wanted to share something on launch day because it's the kind of tool I think
[a specific detail about their role — e.g. "your analytics team at X"] might
actually find useful.

Over the last 60 days I've been in the AB Talks Claude AI challenge and
today I'm launching my capstone. It's called Kickoff — you paste a vague
exec question ("why is retention down?", "should we launch X?") and it
runs a short AI interview then produces a structured analysis brief you
can send back to the exec for scope alignment BEFORE running the analysis.

Basically it turns the "I don't actually know what this exec is asking me
for" problem into a 5-minute exercise instead of a wasted day.

Free, no signup: https://kickoff-5r0.pages.dev
Example brief: https://kickoff-5r0.pages.dev/b/retention

Would love your take — either as an analyst yourself or as someone who
sends analysts these kinds of questions. Even a one-liner reaction is
gold on launch day.

Hope [something specific — new role, recent post, etc.] is going well.
```

**Ask:** one-line reaction from their perspective.

## TEMPLATE C — Cold-ish outreach (5 people — analytics/ops folks you follow but haven't met)

Send via LinkedIn DM or Twitter DM. Short. Assume they don't owe you a response.

```
Hi [Name] — long-time follower of your work on [specific thing they've
posted about that connects to the pain point].

Launching a small free tool today aimed at exactly that problem — Kickoff
turns vague exec questions into structured analysis briefs in 5 minutes.
Free, no signup: https://kickoff-5r0.pages.dev

Not asking for a share. If it's genuinely useful to you, I'd just love
to know — and if it isn't, I'd love to know what's missing that would
make it useful. Either way, thanks for the work you put out publicly.

Example brief so you can judge in 30 seconds:
https://kickoff-5r0.pages.dev/b/retention
```

**Ask:** honest verdict (useful or not-yet-useful, with what's missing).

## RULES

1. **No mass-send.** Send them one at a time. If you get lazy and paste-and-blast, people can tell.
2. **Personalize the first line minimum.** Reference something specific to them.
3. **Never send the same message twice.** If someone asks a follow-up, answer thoughtfully.
4. **Track responses in a spreadsheet.** Name / template used / response / follow-up needed / next step.
5. **Follow up 4 days later** with anyone who engaged but didn't try the product. One follow-up only, then leave it.
6. **Do NOT ask for testimonials on Day 1.** Ask on Day 4 if someone uses it and reacts positively.
